Conclusion (translation of the 7 numbered points)

The study was performed on material from three paraffin blocks of different tumour foci:

  1. All three samples show the baseline oncogenic mutation — a deletion in exon 11 of the KIT gene.
  2. In paraffin block №4222-2/26 (trepan biopsy of the tumour focus that was not removed during the operation of 21.10.2026), an additional mutation in exon 13 of KIT was detected: V654A.
  3. In paraffin blocks №4488-14/26 and №4488-17/26 (different foci of active tumour growth in the liver resection specimen of 21.10.2026), an additional mutation in exon 17 of KIT was detected: N822K.
  4. Thus secondary polyclonal heterogeneity is present: different metastatic foci have different mutational profiles. Both detected mutations in exons 13 and 17 of KIT are markers of resistance to imatinib. Treatment approaches for tumours with exon 13 versus exon 17 mutations also differ. It should be taken into account that the tumour focus remaining in the liver carries a secondary mutation in exon 13 (KIT V654A).
  5. Across these three samples, the exon 18 KIT A829P mutation previously detected in circulating tumour DNA (according to results provided from another laboratory) was not detected. This mutation is likely present in some other focus of active tumour growth in the liver resection specimen whose tissue was not tested for mutational status.
  6. No clinically significant mutations were detected in the other genes examined, including PDGFRA (see gene list).
  7. This diagnostic panel does not provide determination of tumour mutational burden (TMB) or microsatellite status (MSI/MSS).

Technical caveats and items to clarify

  • FACT / INTERPRETATION 5 % VAF reporting threshold & exon 18 codon 829 coverage — RESOLVED (23.11.2026): the written report filters out variants below 5 % VAF, and the earlier hotspot list named exon 18 codons E839/W853 rather than A829. Independent raw-BAM inspection (samtools mpileup -Q 13 on merged.bam.ptrim.bam, hg19) confirmed that amplicon AMPL7170118675 covers KIT codon 829 (c.2485G>C, chr4:55602694) at 2,513×–4,352× depth across all three blocks with 0 / 9,965 alt reads total (0 / 3,100 in 4222-2/26; 0 / 2,513 in 4488-14/26; 0 / 4,352 in 4488-17/26). A829P is genuinely absent in all three tested blocks and not an artifact of low coverage or the 5 % reporting threshold. Cross-block bleed check also showed V654A at background noise (0.20–0.22 %) in 4488-14/17 and N822K at 0.40 % (14 / 3,485, strand-biased +2/−12) in 4222-2/26, consistent with same-chip Ion Torrent barcode bleed on Her-HS-run11.
  • INTERPRETATION Very high VAFs (85.9–92.3 %) in the resected High Grade foci (4488-14 and 4488-17): in diploid tumour cells at 95 % purity, a heterozygous variant is expected near ~47.5 % VAF. Values of 86–92 % for both exon 11 and exon 17 N822K indicate probable loss of the wild-type KIT allele (LOH) or copy-number amplification of the double-mutant allele in cis — a known feature of high-grade imatinib-resistant GIST. (In 4222-2/26 at 40 % purity, VAFs of 23.5 % and 20.7 % match a near-clonal heterozygous exon 11 + exon 13 V654A population.)
  • INTERPRETATION Why N822K was absent from August 2026 blood ctDNA: possible explanations include low ctDNA shedding from the thick-walled cystic right-lobe lesion before surgery, or rapid expansion of the N822K foci between August and October 2026; notably, block 4488-14 also had Ki-67 focally 15 % and High Grade mitotic activity.

Site reading and open questions

INTERPRETATION Everything in this block is this site's inference for discussion with the treating doctors; none of it is a treatment recommendation.

Clinical significance for systemic therapy and surgical planning

  • The remaining known lesion has a different secondary mutation from the resected foci. Both tested active foci in the removed right hemiliver carried the activation-loop mutation exon 17 N822K (which confers resistance to sunitinib and sensitivity to regorafenib/ripretinib). By contrast, the biopsied core of the remaining unresected left-lobe lesion (4222-2/26) carries the ATP-binding-pocket mutation exon 13 V654A.
  • Drug-sensitivity shift if activation-loop clones were largely removed: in clinical subgroup data (INTRIGUE), sunitinib has markedly stronger activity against exon 11 + exon 13/14 (V654A) tumours (median PFS 15.0 months) than against activation-loop exon 17/18 clones (median PFS 2.3 months), whereas ripretinib has reduced activity against exon 13/14 (median PFS 4.0 months vs 14.2 months in exon 17/18) and regorafenib has intermediate activity across both (median PFS 7.4 months in ATP-pocket, 6.7 months in activation loop in VOYAGER). If the resected right hemiliver removed the activation-loop clones (N822K and potentially A829P), sunitinib vs regorafenib becomes a central oncology reassessment — balanced against the patient's January 2023 PRES with left occipital haemorrhage on pazopanib (both sunitinib and regorafenib carry high VEGFR/hypertension/PRES risk) and the timing of a possible second-stage liver resection.
  • Single trepan core caveat: block 4222-2/26 represents one needle-biopsy track from a 4.5 × 4.0 cm heterogeneous lesion; intralesional heterogeneity (e.g., an unsampled A829P or N822K subclone inside the left-lobe mass) cannot be excluded from one biopsy core alone.
