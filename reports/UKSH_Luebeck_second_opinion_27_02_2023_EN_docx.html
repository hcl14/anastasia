History of the disease: 9–12/2022 application of 4 courses of gemcitabine/docetaxel/avastine; 9/2022 [sic] start with pazopanib, development of PRES; 2/2023 application of Yondelis planned, after 3–4 cycles restaging.

27.2.23: Independent recommendation regarding further treatment options

Application of Yondelis if at least stable disease after 3–4 cycles.

If progression, the combination of doxorubicin plus DTIC should be considered — due to the better clinical data vs. doxorubicin mono.

Ifosfamide should be avoided showing negative impact on PFS (and probably OS) by leiomyosarcoma in some retrospective data analysis.

Off label but potentially efficient could be the combination nivolumab/ipilimumab showing some promising disease stabilizations.

ICD: C55 malignant neoplasm of the uterus, part unspecified. (Note: the diagnosis was revised to GIST in May 2023; the two attached publications in the same Drive folder are the references for the immunotherapy suggestion.)
