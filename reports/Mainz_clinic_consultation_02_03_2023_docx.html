Summary of the discussion:

Third-line therapy with Trabectedin was initiated for hepatic metastatic leiomyosarcoma of the uterus (previously Gemcitabine + Docetaxel, Pazopanib --> discontinued at PRES).

External imaging:

MRI skull: Changes in the frontoparietal right and occipital on both sides with increasing occipital hemorrhage without contrast medium uptake. Very compatible with PRES, unlikely to metastasize.

CT: Large right-hepatic metastasis of constant size, left-hepatic metastasis and large tumor nodules in the left epigastrium gradually regressing. Pelvic images only partially transferred.

Recommendation:
