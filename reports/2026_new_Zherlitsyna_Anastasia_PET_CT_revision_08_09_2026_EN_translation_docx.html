Expert opinion on FDG PET-CT — English translation

Patient and study details

Patient: ZHERLITZINA ANASTASIA / Zherlitsyna Anastasia

ID: ME923960

Date of birth: 25.04.1976

Study date: 19.06.2026

Date of reading: 08.09.2026

Indication

The patient is under follow-up and receiving treatment for metastatic gastrointestinal stromal tumour (GIST).

Comparison: with the previous study of 13.05.2025.

Technique

The study was performed after intravenous injection of the radiopharmaceutical, without intravenous and without oral contrast agent.

Findings

Head and neck

As is known, the diagnostic capability of this study with respect to the brain is limited. Within the limits of the method, no gross changes of the brain parenchyma are identified.

No hypermetabolic enlarged cervical lymph nodes are identified.

The thyroid gland is of normal size, without pathological FDG uptake.

Chest

Small nodules are seen in both lungs, stable over a long period, with sizes below the resolution of PET.

No pleural effusion.

Minimal amount of pericardial fluid — unchanged.

No pathological FDG uptake in the mediastinal and hilar lymph nodes.

No pathological FDG uptake in the breast tissue.

No pathological uptake in the axillary lymph nodes.

The superior vena cava (SVC) is not visualised — probably occluded. Dilated collateral vessels are seen in the chest.

Abdomen and pelvis

The known hypodense lesions are again identified in both lobes of the liver.

A large lesion in the right lobe of the liver with peripheral FDG uptake, which in the present study is somewhat more pronounced. The size of the lesion is about 15 × 10 cm; on the previous study — about 11–12.5 cm.

The lesion in the left lobe of the liver, about 42 × 36 mm — without significant change in size, however the intensity of FDG uptake has now increased.

The gallbladder is not enlarged. The intrahepatic bile ducts are not dilated. The portal vein is patent.

The known lesion in the stomach wall with calcifications, without significant change in size — about 37 × 31 mm. Minimal FDG uptake is noted, similar to that observed previously, without significant dynamics.

The spleen is of normal size, without pathological FDG uptake.

No pathological FDG uptake is identified in the pancreatic parenchyma.

The right adrenal gland is compressed by the liver, without mass lesions and without pathological FDG uptake.

Thickening of the left adrenal gland without pathological FDG uptake — unchanged.

Kidneys without radiopaque calculi, no hydronephrosis.

No hypermetabolic enlarged lymph nodes are identified in the abdominal cavity, retroperitoneum or pelvis.

No pathological FDG uptake in the inguinal lymph nodes.

Urinary bladder without significant changes.

Bowel loops, as far as can be assessed by this method, without significant changes.

The known heterogeneous lesions of the uterus — without significant dynamics.

Skeletal system

Within the examined area no pathological foci of FDG uptake or destructive bone changes are identified.

Conclusion

The known gastric lesion — without significant change in size and without significant FDG uptake.

Metabolic progression of the liver lesions, especially of the lesion in the left lobe, as well as enlargement of the large lesion in the right lobe of the liver, whose metabolic activity has also somewhat increased.

Small pulmonary nodules — unchanged.
