Condition after 4 cycles of chemotherapy according to the Gemcitabine + Docetaxel + Avastin protocol with progression in the liver (MRI of the abdominal cavity, pelvic organs on October 14, 2022, CT scan on December 1, 2022).

Kit, MET mutations were detected (whole genome sequencing ONCOMINE, 12.2022).
