MRI in dynamics from January 20, 2023.

On a series of MR tomograms of the brain, previously described diffuse foci of hyperintense MR signal on T2WI and T2blade dark-fl are preserved in the cortical and subcortical sections of both hemispheres of the brain (mainly in the parietal regions), however, with their significant regression in the form of a decrease in size, less hyperintense MR signal on T2_spc_da-fl_iso and with regression of contrast agent accumulation in their structure. The appearance of new foci is not determined.

The focus of intracerebral hemorrhage is preserved in the left occipital region with general conventional dimensions of 11x7.5x7.5 mm (reduced in size), with signs of diffusion limitation with minimal perifocal swelling of the brain substance, up to 3 mm wide; a small focus of post-hemorrhagic changes in the right occipital region is also determined.

Along the course of the subarachnoid spaces in the frontal regions, there are areas of hypointense MR signal on SWI, increased on T2WI and T2_spc_da-fl_iso, which are not defined on DWI and do not accumulate contrast agent (perhaps the consequences of local SAH or hemorrhagic impregnation).

Middle structures without lateral displacement. The lateral ventricles of the brain are symmetrical, unchanged in size, the IV ventricle is located along the midline. The demarcation of gray and white matter is clear.

Periventricular zones of leukoaraiosis with a width of up to 2 mm.

The corpus callosum, basal ganglia, mesencephalic structures, structures of the posterior cranial fossa, brain stem do not contain areas of pathological changes in the MR signal.

Craniovertebral junction: cerebellar tonsils without signs of prolapse into the large occipital foramen.

The sella turcica: located typically, conventional configuration. The walls are even, the contours are smooth, clear. The pituitary stalk is located clearly in the center. The pituitary gland is bean-shaped, the upper edge is symmetrical.

Chiasma, optic nerves without features.

Pneumatization of the paranasal sinuses is not disturbed.

Conclusion: the MR picture of pathological changes in the brain, which have significantly decreased in intensity and prevalence in comparison with the MRI examination from 01.20.2023, taking into account the data of the clinic and history, previous examinations, localization and MR characteristics, may correspond to the consequences of posterior reversible encephalopathy syndrome (PRES). In comparison with the previous MRI examination from January 20, 2023, a decrease in the size and transformation of the contents of the intracerebral hemorrhage in the left occipital lobe of the brain is determined.

Expedient: clinical and tomographic comparison, MR control in dynamics, observation of a neurologist.
