  • In a clinical case study, treatment with Crizotinib resulted in a response with 74% tumor size reduction and clinical benefit for more than 8 months in a patient with NSCLC and KIF5B-MET fusion (PMID: 29284707).

MOLECULAR EVIDENCE LEVEL

• 2nd therapeutic approach

Multikinase Inhibitor m2A

  • KIT Q556 V559delinsH results in the deletion of 4 amino acids in the juxtamembrane domain (exon 11) of the Kit protein combined with the insertion of a histidine (H) at the same spot (PMID: 16226710). Q556_V559delinsH has not been characterized, however, similar Kit exon 11 deletions are activated (PMID: 9438854, PMID: 15365079) and are therefore predicted to lead to an enhancement of Kit protein function

  • lamtinib/dasatinib/nilotinib/ripretinib is approved in melanoma with KIT mutations.

  • Imatinib/Sunitinib is included in the guidelines for patients with GIST with sensitizing mutations such as KIT exon 11 mutations as adjuvant therapy for patients with localized disease and as first-line therapy for patients with locally advanced, unresectable, or metastatic disease (PMID: 34560242; ESMO. org).

  • In a clinical case study, treatment with Imatinib resulted in SD lasting at least 3 years in a patient with extraskeletal myxoid chondrosarcoma carrying KIT W557G (PMID: 34446510).

  • In a phase II study, treatment with Nilotinib resulted in an ORR of 26.2% (11/42) in melanoma patients with KIT mutations, including 11 patients with PR, an mDOR of 7.1 months, and an OS of 18 months (PMID: 28327988).

  • In KIT mutations, sunitinib and imatinib showed good efficacy in GIST and sarcoma.

The combination of a molecularly targeted therapy with other systemic or local therapy modalities is at the discretion of the treating physician and is not ruled out by the Tumor Board recommendation.

Evidence level

  • m1A In the same tumor entity, the predictive value of the biomarker or clinical efficacy was demonstrated in a biomarker-stratified cohort of an adequately powered prospective study or meta-analysis.

  • m1B In the same tumor entity, the predictive value of the biomarker or clinical efficacy was demonstrated in a retrospective cohort or case-control study.

  • m2A In another tumor entity, the predictive value of the biomarker or clinical efficacy was demonstrated in a biomarker-stratified cohort of an adequately powered prospective study or meta-analysis.

  • m2B In another tumor entity, the predictive value of the biomarker or clinical efficacy was demonstrated in a retrospective cohort or case-control study.

  • m2C Irrespective of the tumor entity, clinical efficacy has been demonstrated in one or more case reports in the presence of the biomarker.

  • m3 Preclinical data (in vitro/in vivo models, functional studies) show an association of the biomarker with the efficacy of the medication, which is supported by a scientific rationale.

  • m4 A scientific, biological rationale suggests an association of the biomarker with the efficacy of the medication, which is not yet supported by (pre-)clinical data.

  • R1 Evidence for therapy resistance from clinical trials in the same entity

  • R2 Evidence for therapy resistance from clinical studies in different entities or from in-vitro investigations

  • Prefix u: Unclear evidence with contradictory study results, the evidence is given that is clinically most likely (comparison of the quality of the studies: prospective phase III, ...)
