explained by a single cause.

Mutations in the studied genes lead to certain cases of hereditary tumors, but mutations in
other genes can also increase the risk of developing cancer. Especially, if you have a personal
or family history of cancer, you can consider the option of additional genetic research that
studies a larger number of genes associated with hereditary forms of cancer. Your doctor can
help you determine whether you need further testing.
