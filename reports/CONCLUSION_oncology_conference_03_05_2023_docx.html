Anastasia Stanislavivna Zherlitsina

Apr 25, 1976

ID: 110889

Diagnosis: Leiomyosarcoma of the uterine body, T2NxM1, secondary liver, peritoneum damage.

Condition after 4 cycles of chemotherapy according to the Gemcitabine + Docetaxel + Avastin protocol with progression of the disease in the liver (according to CT, MRI from 10/2022). C-KIT, MET (ONCOMINE) mutation was detected. The condition after treatment with Pazopanib (05-12/01/2023) with the development of the posterior reversible encephalopathy syndrome and positive dynamics according to the CT data.

01/2023 - repeated liver biopsy. Pathohistological conclusion - uterine leiomyosarcoma metastasis. Condition after 3 cycles of chemotherapy according to the Trabectedin protocol with negative X-ray dynamics (PET/CT from 11/04/2023). Suspicion of the presence of synchronous neoplasia of the stomach (GIST?). Condition after EUS-FNA of gastric biopsy, liver core biopsy, histologically - gastrointestinal stromal tumor (GIST) of the stomach, GIST metastasis in the liver (CSD).

Recommended:

Start treatment with Imatinib at a dose of 400 mg per day with condition control (clinical examination + CT scan in comparison) after 3 months of treatment.

Revision of histological preparations from 08/2022 in the reference laboratory (Frankfurt/CSD) for the purpose of differential diagnosis of uterine leiomyosarcoma and GIST.

Evaluating follow-up tactics based on FoundationOne research results.
