The patient Zherlitsina A.S., born in 1976, was treated from February 9 to 15, 2023 in the inpatient conditions of the "Innovation" clinic.

Diagnosis: Leiomyosarcoma of the uterus T2NxM1 (liver, peritoneum).

Condition after 4 cycles of chemotherapy according to the Gemcitabine + Docetaxel + Avastin protocol with progression in the liver (MRI of the abdominal cavity, pelvic organs on October 14, 2022, CT scan on December 1, 2022).

Kit, MET mutations were detected (whole genome sequencing ONCOMINE, 12.2022).

Treatment with Pazopanib from January 5 to 12, 2023, with the development of the posterior reversible encephalopathy syndrome and positive dynamics according to CT data from February 9, 2023. Conclusions: CT signs of secondary damage to the liver parenchyma, interloop implant of the left mesogastric, volume formation in the projection of the right ovary (implant in the Douglas space?), minimal signs of biliary hypertension. From January 4, 2023, the dynamics are positive - regression of foci in the liver by 12%, decrease in the size of the interloop implant by 15%.

Associated diagnosis: Iron deficiency anemia. Type 2 diabetes mellitus, hypovitaminosis D.
