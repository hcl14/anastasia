7. Brief history, diagnostic investigations, course of the disease, treatment given, condition on referral and at discharge

Complaints: general weakness. History of the present illness: ill since 08.2022, when multiple bilobar liver lesions, a paragastric mass and a pelvic mass were found. The process was initially regarded as metastatic leiomyosarcoma of the uterus, for which she received systemic therapy with gemcitabine, docetaxel, bevacizumab, later pazopanib and trabectedin, without a durable effect. After repeat biopsies and review of the histological material in 2023 the diagnosis was revised to GIST of the stomach with metastatic involvement of the liver. From 03.05.2023 she received imatinib 400 mg/day with a marked metabolic response and stabilisation of the disease until 2025. From 06.2026, PET-CT showed progression of the metastatic liver involvement. Molecular testing of 08.2026 found a primary KIT exon 11 mutation and secondary KIT exon 13 and exon 18 mutations, consistent with resistance to imatinib. PET-CT of 23.09.2026: further metabolic progression of multiple liver foci. Locoregional and surgical treatment methods were considered; thermal ablation of the left-lobe focus was judged technically inadvisable. On 30.09.2026 regorafenib 160 mg/day was prescribed; treatment had not been started at the time of admission. Admitted electively to department IV of the NSCST for further work-up and to decide the further treatment strategy. Copies of all investigations are attached to the case record.

Main investigations: 30.09.2026 complete blood count, blood biochemistry — see the case record.
