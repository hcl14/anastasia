Clinical question:

Malignant uterine tumor. Posterior reversible encephalopathy syndrome (PRES) as a side effect of therapy with Votrient.

MRI of the head from 20.01.2023, 20.09.2022:

Measurement technique: sagittal T1 MPRAGE 1 mm, axial DWI, axial 3D-TOF-MRA axial T2 TSE, coronary TWIST, axial T1 MPRAGE 1 mm after contrast administration

Conclusion:

Therapy-associated changes with parieto-occipital diffusion disorders, occipital hemorrhages and constriction of cerebral arteries, including the middle cerebral artery on both sides. These changes are a typical consequence of therapy with tyrosine kinase inhibitors such as Votrient (and can be subsumed under the umbrella term PRES, although narrowing of the arteries is not consistent with PRES).
