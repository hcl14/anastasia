Therapy and progress:

The CT and MRT follow-up was carried out. Here, advanced abdominal tumor development is evident. Furthermore, according to the medical history of a PRES 01/2023, there was a residual hemorrhagic defect on the left occipital of approx. 1 cm.

We discussed the available imaging in our sarcoma conference in synopsis of the submitted external reports. Unfortunately, due to a lack of data, it was not possible to compare our imaging presentation with preliminary examinations, so that an assessment of the course after around 6 weeks of Imatinib therapy could not be made. From the point of view of our colleagues in radiology, however, the image morphology of the appearance of a GIST of the stomach plus hepatic and peritoneal metastases is very compatible with the current revision of the histology. The tumor findings on the uterus do not allow any further classification on the basis of CT morphology, but from our point of view the differentiation of these structures from the tumor findings of the upper abdomen in the context of PET/CT appears with a. e. Leiomyomas of the uterus understandable. Unfortunately, the external PET/CT was not available to us in digitized form, so that only the written report was evaluated here.

Likewise, no independent histological or immunohistochemical assessment of the externally removed tumor material could be carried out. At this point, too, our assessment was based on the submitted external reports, which we also judged to be conclusive.

We therefore recommended continuation of therapy with Imatinib. After a detailed discussion of our assessment, the patient rejected the immediately offered options for a new punch biopsy and histological analysis. However, she will endeavor to make the materials already taken available to our clinic for reference pathological assessment.
