In the projection of foci, an increase in the signal on DWI at b-400-800 with diffusion limitation is determined on the ADC map,

Bile ducts are not dilated. Gallbladder is not changed, does not contain concretions.

Retroperitoneal lymphadenopathy, hepatoduodenal lymphadenopathy was not found.

On CT- and MRI-scans of pelvic organs:

Uterus in anteflexion/anteversion. The body of the uterus is 47x48x50 mm, the outer contour is clear, the cavity is not expanded, the endometrium is 3 mm, total sagittal. Signs of diffuse adenomyosis are determined;

intramural myomas in the stage of fibrous degeneration, up to 23x19 mm in size, are determined. Dilated parametrium vessels are determined.

A tumor node on the leg is determined along the front contour of the uterus, max. axial dimensions

86x62mm (series 501000, scan 15), craniocaudal process size - 63mm (series 201000, scan 13). The structure of the formation is solid, heterogeneous, with signs of malignancy, the outer contour is clear.

The cervix is 26x34x24mm, the MR signal from the cervix has not changed.

In the projection of the right ovary - a cystic formation measuring 69x63x53mm, filled with hemorrhagic contents.

Bladder of normal filling, walls of uniform thickness.

The rectum is unremarkable.

MR signal from bones is not changed.

CONCLUSION

Radiological signs of sarcoma of the body of the uterus (node on the leg). "M" lesion of the left ovary. "M" - damage to the liver. Sarcomatosis of the abdominal cavity (solitary node in the projection of the left parts of the epigastrium). Endometrioid cyst of the right appendix.
