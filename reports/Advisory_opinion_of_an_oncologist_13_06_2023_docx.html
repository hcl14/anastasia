Condition after EUS-FNA of stomach biopsy (April 21, 2023), liver core biopsy (April 25, 2023), histologically - gastrointestinal stromal tumor (GIST) of the stomach, GIST metastasis in the liver (CSD).

Condition after trepan biopsy of the liver (August 28, 2022, NIR). Histopathological conclusion: leiomyosarcoma of the uterus.

Condition after 4 cycles of chemotherapy according to the Gemcitabine+Docetaxel+Avastin protocol with progression in the liver (MRI of the abdominal cavity, pelvic organs 14.10.2022, CT scan 01.12.2022).

Mutations of Kit, MET were detected (whole genome sequencing ONCOMINE, 12.2022).

Treatment with Pazopanib 05-12.01.2023. with the development of the posterior reverse encephalopathy syndrome and positive dynamics according to CT data from 09.02.2023.

Repeat liver biopsy (January 23, 2023). Histopathological conclusion: metastasis of uterine leiomyosarcoma.

The condition after 3 cycles of chemotherapy according to the Trabectedin protocol with minimal negative dynamics according to the PET-CT data (11.04.2023): Known masses in the liver and the mass on the left, which according to the configuration rather originates from the stomach wall have slightly increased, are metabolically active. Masses in the uterus and in/near the right ovary with low metabolic activity, structurally stable. A significant difference in the activity of liver/stomach and uterine masses suggests different neoplastic processes, in particular, primary aggressive from the stomach/GIST.
