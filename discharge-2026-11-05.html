INTERPRETATION The oncologist's dose, 160 mg once daily for 21 days, matches the approved regorafenib schedule of 3 weeks on and 1 week off (US prescribing information). The label carries a boxed warning for severe liver toxicity and advises liver tests before starting and at least every 2 weeks during the first 2 months. This matters after a major liver resection. Whether imatinib stops when regorafenib starts is for the treating oncologist. The surgeon earlier preferred PET-CT with IV contrast for the first post-operative scan (28.10.2026); the discharge recommends a contrast CT.
